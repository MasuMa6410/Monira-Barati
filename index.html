02 / A little about me

Good design is
clear, not loud.

I'm Monira, a graphic designer interested in the space between a strong idea and a clear visual message.

My work explores visual identity, social media design, and print. I enjoy bringing structure to ideas through typography, thoughtful color, and details that make a design feel intentional. I'm always learning, experimenting, and looking for ways to make my work more useful and more considered.

03 / My approach

Thoughtful from
first idea to final detail.

A simple process keeps each design grounded in its purpose, not just its appearance.

01

Understand

Clarify the audience, purpose, message, and practical needs before choosing a visual direction.

02

Explore

Test references, typography, color, and composition to find a direction that feels right.

03

Refine

Improve hierarchy, spacing, and consistency through feedback and careful iteration.

04

Deliver

Prepare polished, purposeful designs in the formats needed for their real-world use.
